## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Engineering-led SEO — technical fixes, programmatic pages and AI-assisted content systems that grow organic traffic you can measure.

**Who it's for:**
- SaaS, marketplaces and content sites where scale (hundreds or thousands of pages) matters.
- Businesses whose site has technical problems blocking rankings.
- Teams that want a content engine, not a monthly blog-post package.

**What they fear:** paying monthly for "SEO" with no visible work, AI-spam content that gets penalized, losing traffic in a migration, vague reports, black-hat tactics.

**Where Faraz wins:** AI SEO Automator — he built the automation himself. A developer can fix technical SEO in the code, build programmatic page systems, and run quality-controlled AI content pipelines.

## 5. Copy direction

**Headline options:**
1. "SEO built into your code, not bolted on."
2. "Organic growth, engineered."
3. "From technical fixes to content at scale — measured in Search Console, not promises."

**Use:** indexed, crawlable, programmatic, measured, human-reviewed.
**Avoid:** "rank #1 guaranteed", "page one in 30 days", black-hat language.

**FAQ:** How long until results? · Is AI content safe for rankings? · Do you build links? · Can you work in my CMS / framework? · What do monthly reports show? · Will a migration hurt traffic?

---

## 6. Design direction

**Concept: "SERP."** Data-forward and clear — charts and search results as the visual language.

- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono for metrics and URLs.
- **Colour:** off-white `#F5F5F0`, ink text, signal yellow `#FFD60A` highlighter accent (like marking a result), chart blues/greys. Light theme.
- **Motion:** graph lines draw; search-result cards climb positions; highlighter sweeps across key phrases.
- **Mobile:** chart simplified with annotations as cards beneath.
- **Brand thread:** footer signature in Clash Display.
