## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Shopify development for brands that outgrew their theme — faster stores, custom features and integrations that sync everything.

**Who it's for:**
- DTC brands doing roughly $20k–$2M/month on Shopify.
- Multi-channel sellers syncing Shopify with Amazon, eBay, marketplaces.

**What they fear:** slow stores losing sales, app bloat and monthly app fees, broken themes after updates, inventory out of sync, developers who disappear.

**Where Faraz wins:** Yard Terminal — he built a system that syncs Shopify with eBay, Amazon, Google and Facebook Marketplace. That's integration depth most theme developers don't have.

## 5. Copy direction

**Headline options:**
1. "A faster Shopify store with fewer apps."
2. "Shopify, built for brands that outgrew the theme."
3. "Sell everywhere. Manage it in one place."

**Use:** speed, conversion, fewer apps, in sync, editable.
**Avoid:** "6-figure store", guaranteed revenue claims.

**FAQ:** Theme customization or full rebuild? · Can you speed up my current store? · Will my team be able to edit it? · Do you do Shopify Plus? · Which marketplaces can you sync? · Will the launch disrupt sales?

---

## 6. Design direction

**Concept: "Storefront."** Editorial retail: product-photography energy, warm and premium.

- **Type:** Gambetta (serif display), Satoshi (body), JetBrains Mono for metrics.
- **Colour:** warm paper `#F6F1E9`, ink text, terracotta `#D9532B` accent. Light theme. Avoid Shopify's green.
- **Motion:** product cards slide like a shelf; speed meter fills; sync map lines pulse with order "packets".
- **Mobile:** teardown becomes a swipeable card stack.
- **Brand thread:** footer signature in Clash Display.
