## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Marketing automation that turns leads into booked calls on its own — email, SMS and CRM flows built and measured by an engineer.

**Who it's for:**
- B2B service firms, agencies, coaches and consultants with leads but weak follow-up.
- Businesses with a CRM full of contacts nobody nurtures.

**What they fear:** leads going cold, spammy sequences that hurt the brand, tools that don't talk to each other, not knowing which emails make money.

**Where Faraz wins:** Lead Drift (his own outreach company/system) is first-hand proof; he can connect any tool, track attribution properly (his own lead pipeline captures landing page, referrer and UTMs end to end) and add AI personalization.

## 5. Copy direction

**Headline options:**
1. "Follow-up that never forgets a lead."
2. "Turn the leads you already have into booked calls."
3. "Marketing automation, engineered — not templated."

**Use:** follow-up, booked calls, deliverability, attribution, measured.
**Avoid:** "growth hacking", "explode your sales", income claims.

**FAQ:** Which tools (HubSpot, GHL, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo…)? · Will this hurt my email reputation? · Do you write the copy? · How fast do results show? · Who owns the lists and flows? · GDPR / CAN-SPAM?

---

## 6. Design direction

**Concept: "Flow."** Warm, human, conversational — messages moving through time.

- **Type:** Cabinet Grotesk (display), Satoshi (body), JetBrains Mono for timestamps.
- **Colour:** cream `#F7F2EA`, ink text, rose `#FF3D7F` accent, soft message-bubble greys. Light theme.
- **Motion:** the lead travels along a curved path; message bubbles pop in on schedule; stage counter increments; Lenis smooth scroll.
- **Mobile:** vertical timeline with sticky day labels.
- **Brand thread:** footer signature in Clash Display.
