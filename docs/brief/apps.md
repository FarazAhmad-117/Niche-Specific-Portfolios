## 1. Positioning

**One line:** A senior full-stack engineer who takes a product from spec to shipped — MVPs, SaaS and APIs built to grow, not to be rewritten.

**Who it's for:**
- Non-technical founders building an MVP or v2.
- Startups and small companies needing a senior builder without hiring full-time.
- Businesses replacing spreadsheets or a tangle of tools with one internal app.

**What they fear:** agencies that vanish mid-build, scope creep and blown budgets, code nobody else can maintain, no access to their own repo, an MVP that can't scale.

**Where Faraz wins:** hard, real products in the portfolio (text-to-CAD, multi-marketplace commerce engine), plus AI inside products — not just CRUD apps.

## 5. Copy direction

**Headline options:**
1. "Products that ship — and keep shipping."
2. "From spec to production, built by one senior engineer."
3. "Your MVP, built like it's v3."

**Use:** shipped, weekly demos, your repo, fixed scope, built to scale.
**Avoid:** "pixel-perfect", "cutting-edge", "full-service".

**FAQ:** Fixed price or time-and-materials? · Do I own the code? · What stack and why? · Can you take over an existing codebase? · How do you handle scope changes? · What happens after launch? · Can you work with my designer / team?

---

## 6. Design direction

**Concept: "Blueprint."** Technical drawings that turn into real interfaces — precision with craft.

- **Type:** Satoshi (display/body), IBM Plex Mono for annotations and dimensions.
- **Colour:** off-white paper `#F3F1EA`, ink lines, safety orange `#FF4F00` accent for "built" states. Light theme with dark case-study sections.
- **Motion:** SVG blueprint lines draw, then fill into UI screenshots; dimension annotations slide in; deploy log types at the end.
- **Mobile:** blueprint sequence becomes stacked frames with a progress rail.
- **Brand thread:** footer signature in Clash Display.
