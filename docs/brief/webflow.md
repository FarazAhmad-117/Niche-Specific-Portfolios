## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Webflow sites that look premium and that your marketing team can run alone — design, interactions, CMS and integrations, built by a developer.

**Who it's for:**
- SaaS and B2B startups needing a marketing site the team can update without devs.
- Agencies and designers needing a developer to build their Figma designs in Webflow.
- Companies migrating from WordPress.

**What they fear:** a beautiful site nobody can edit, broken CMS structure, slow heavy interactions, migration losing SEO, custom needs Webflow "can't do".

**Where Faraz wins:** a real developer behind Webflow — custom code, APIs, CMS syncing, forms to CRM, and GSAP-level motion (which this very portfolio demonstrates).

## 6. Design direction

**Concept: "Canvas."** A design-studio site: bold type, generous space, motion as the showcase.

- **Type:** Unbounded (display), Satoshi (body), JetBrains Mono for class names and specs.
- **Colour:** white `#FFFFFF`, ink, violet `#6B4CFF` accent, pale grey panels. Light theme. Avoid Webflow's blue.
- **Motion:** the most expressive of all niche sites — component assembly, page-transition demos, cursor-reactive elements. Still respects reduced motion and the performance budget.
- **Mobile:** assembly sequence becomes stacked frames; gallery becomes a swipe carousel.
- **Brand thread:** footer signature in Clash Display.
